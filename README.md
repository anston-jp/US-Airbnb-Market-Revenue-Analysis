# 🏡 US Airbnb Market & Revenue Analysis

## Overview
This project presents a comprehensive visual analysis of the US Airbnb market using Tableau. By exploring listing prices, property types, location-based performance, and room configurations, this dashboard provides actionable insights for real estate investors, hosts, and property managers aiming to optimize rental strategy and maximize revenue.

## Dataset
- **File Name:** `AirBnb_us_data.xlsx`
- **Scope:** US-based Airbnb listings data containing information on pricing, number of bedrooms, zip codes, property types, and guest capacities.

## Business Objectives
1. **Pricing Dynamic Analysis:** Identify how listing prices scale with property size (number of bedrooms) and room types.
2. **Geographic Revenue Distribution:** Determine top-performing zip codes and regions based on average price and revenue potential.
3. **Property Portfolio Optimization:** Provide data-backed recommendations on optimal property configurations for potential investors.

---

## Key Findings
* **Bedroom Configuration vs. Price:** Average price per night increases predictably with bedroom count, with 4–5 bedroom properties yielding higher average revenue per listing.
* **Zip Code Variation:** Pricing varies significantly across zip codes, highlighting distinct premium micro-markets versus budget-friendly suburban regions.
* **Room Type Dominance:** "Entire Home/Apartment" listings command a substantial premium over private and shared rooms, making them the primary revenue driver across all regions.

---

## Executive Summary
The US Airbnb market exhibits strong location-dependent and listing-type pricing power. Maximizing returns depends heavily on property selection: large, multi-bedroom entire homes situated in high-demand zip codes generate the highest yield. Investors targeting maximum gross revenue should prioritize properties capable of accommodating larger groups in top-tier zip codes rather than high-density small listings in lower-tier markets.

---

## Recommendations
1. **Target Multi-Bedroom Investments:** Focus on purchasing 3-to-4 bedroom properties, as they balance lower acquisition competition with elevated average nightly rates.
2. **Location-Centric Strategy:** Allocate capital into top-performing zip codes identified in the dashboard to secure higher baseline occupancy and premium pricing.
3. **Listing Optimization:** Convert shared or private space listings into full "Entire Place" rentals whenever feasible to leverage higher consumer willingness to pay.

---

## Limitations
* **Snapshot Data:** The analysis relies on static listing data rather than dynamic historical booking trends over multiple years.
* **Cost Constraints:** Net profitability metrics are limited because operational costs (cleaning, maintenance, local taxes, HOA fees) were not included in the dataset.
* **Estimated Occupancy:** Revenue figures assume baseline availability and review proxies rather than exact internal financial transactions.
